Seoul, South Korea 2022

These are works from when I was around 20 years old. I was going through a rebellious phase at the time. I don't think I could take photographs like these anymore. Back then, I liked photographing things in dark, muted tones. I think I didn't really understand what sadness was supposed to feel like back then. Or maybe I understood it all too well, and compared to who I was then, I've simply become too numb now. I can't really tell which one it is. These days, when I'm sad, all I want to do is sleep.
